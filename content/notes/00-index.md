## Conventions

- Scalars are lowercase italic ($x$), vectors are bold lowercase ($\mathbf{x}$), matrices are bold uppercase ($\mathbf{X}$).
- $m$ is the number of training examples and $n$ the number of features.
- A hat means "predicted": $\hat{y}$ is the model's estimate of $y$.
